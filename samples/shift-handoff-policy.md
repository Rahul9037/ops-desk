# Shift handoff policy

Ops Desk uses this note for on-call handoff. It is the only source for the rules below.

## Severity

A Sev-1 incident pages the incident commander within 5 minutes.
A Sev-2 incident is acknowledged within 30 minutes during the on-call shift.
A Sev-3 incident waits for the next business day and is not paged overnight.
Customer-visible data loss is always Sev-1, even outside business hours.

## Handoff

The outgoing on-call writes a handoff note before 09:00 local time.
The note lists open incidents, the current severity, and who owns the next action.
A handoff is incomplete if it does not name an owner.

## Escalation

If a Sev-1 is still unowned after 15 minutes, escalate to the ops manager.
The ops manager for this rotation is Priya Raman.
Do not page the finance team for an incident.
